\begin{letter}{Editors, npj Quantum Information}
\opening{Dear Editors,}

We submit the enclosed manuscript, ``Certified Adversarial Risk Bounds for
Quantum Error-Correcting Memories: Injection-Budget Certification with
Simultaneous Intervals and Reproducible Three-Code Bivariate-Bicycle
Benchmarks,'' for consideration in npj Quantum Information.

The manuscript reframes quantum memory evaluation as a certification problem:
for a fixed implementation and a bounded-weight fault-injection adversary, the
operational risk $\lambda_{\mathcal{M}}(W)$ is estimated on device and reported
as simultaneous intervals $[L_W,U_W]$ at joint $\ge 95\%$ confidence, with
upper bounds stated honestly as $1$ wherever the weight region is not
exhausted. Four contributions we believe clear the journal's bar:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=4pt]
\item \textbf{A certification framework with simultaneous intervals and a
   machine-checkable risk certificate.} Intervals are
   Bonferroni-simultaneous across all 676 statistical cells with exact
   Clopper--Pearson bounds at zero counts ($2.996/N$, not the
   anti-conservative Wilson value), and uniform-mixture confidence sequences
   govern the adaptive ladder. Where enumeration is complete --- the $t=2$
   radius and the noiseless injection boundary --- the certified value is
   exact, not a bound; where it is not, the upper bound is stated as $1$
   with the gap printed. The per-system lower/upper envelope is shipped as a
   single machine-checkable artifact (\texttt{risk\_certificate\_v24.csv})
   whose every entry regenerates from the archived counts. Estimands were
   hash-pinned before submission; where a conditioning estimand was instead
   adopted retrospectively (campaign~1), the manuscript says so explicitly
   in a dated chronology ledger. To our knowledge this is the first
   QEC benchmark whose headline numbers are simultaneous intervals with an
   explicit threat model.
\item \textbf{An exhaustive, independently checkable code certificate.}
   BB[[72,12,6]] is certified $d=6$ exactly (meet-in-the-middle through
   weight~5; exhaustive weight-6 enumeration exhibiting $84+84$ nontrivial
   logicals). The archive publishes all witnesses and ships a self-contained
   334-line checker that re-derives every obligation from the pinned matrices,
   so the distance claim is third-party verifiable in linear time per witness.
\item \textbf{A reproducible three-code benchmark at pinned scales.}
   Certification-first, bit-identical paired comparisons across BB72/BB144/BB288:
   BP+OSD lies $67.8\times$ to $>{134{,}000}\times$ below graphified matching
   baselines (joint $\ge95\%$); a seven-decoder grid on 70{,}000 paired
   instances shows BP augmentation closes the gap to BP+OSD while bare symmetry
   matching is non-viable; a five-decoder re-analysis of the released Kunlun
   $[[18,4,4]]$ detector model confirms the graphification penalty on hardware
   matrices; and a prior-aligned repeated-cycle ladder measures block-level
   distance suppression ($\Lambda$ per $\Delta d{=}2$, honestly delimited
   against per-cycle conventions).
\item \textbf{Hash-pinned hardware injection campaigns.} On
   ibm\_marrakesh and ibm\_fez, a pinned injection-budget protocol confirmed
   the distance cliff ($92$--$96\%$ collapse) and the device-aware composed
   bound on every arm, replicating across calibration cycles, devices, and
   distance~7; calibration-informed placement lowered logical error in four of
   four configurations ($p=0.042$). Two v23-era defects found in review --- a
   reversed extraction convention in one circuit-level campaign and a
   population-average estimand misapplied to pinned masks --- are disclosed in
   the manuscript, corrected with protocol-invariance tests and a
   fixed-mask reanalysis, and the affected claims are withdrawn or replaced.
   A second-round review found two further defects --- an endpoint
   classifier keyed to the observed rather than the true residual syndrome,
   and ``pre-registered'' phrasing applied to a retrospectively adopted
   estimand --- which are likewise corrected (regression-tested classifier,
   regenerated tables, and a full chronology ledger) and disclosed.
\end{enumerate}

The package is provenance-anchored: the simulation and analysis figures and
tables regenerate from the accompanying archive (SHA-256 manifests,
deterministic seeds, recorded job IDs and QPY circuits for all hardware runs,
an offline master reproduction script, and pinned dependency versions).
Hardware outcomes themselves are experimental records and are re-attainable
only by re-running the pinned protocols on device, which the archive fully
specifies; the corrected circuit-level grid regenerates rate by rate under a
checkpointed protocol, and the manuscript states precisely which rates are
complete at submission.
